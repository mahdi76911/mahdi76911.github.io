Featured Work

GBDA Net — dehazing that keeps objects detectable

Master's Thesis · Foggy Image Enhancement Framework · 2021–2022

Object-visibility-aware dehazing autoencoder

Most dehazing networks are trained to look pixel-similar to a clean image — but that can erase exactly the detail a downstream detector needs. GBDA Net folds IoU of YOLOv5 detections in the dehazed image directly into the loss, so enhancement preserves the objects that matter for ADAS. A physics-based synthetic-fog generator (from stereo depth) produces training pairs without real fog photography.

Problem

Vehicles in fog are hard to detect; naive dehazing destroys the cues detectors rely on.

Method

Convolutional dehazing autoencoder + haze-model fog generator; 10+ classical & deep baselines.

Eval

KITTI, RESIDE, UAVDT, CARLA; MATLAB/Python no-reference IQA (SSIM, PSNR, UQI, contrast).

Outcome

+22% vehicle detection in fog for ADAS; framework open-sourced; paper submitted.
